## PART A: LEGAL OPINION — REFERRAL PARTNER CLASSIFICATION

### 1. Classification: Independent Contractor

Referral Partners do **not** trigger the deeming provisions under section 200A of the Labour Relations Act 66 of 1995 ("LRA") or section 83A of the Basic Conditions of Employment Act 75 of 1997 ("BCEA"). The analysis differs slightly across the three referral types this Agreement covers — the food store partner referral type involves the most in-person setup work and therefore warrants the most careful scrutiny.

#### 1.1 Customer Referral Types (Food and Furniture)

| Factor (s200A LRA) | Referral Partner Position |
|---|---|
| Control over manner of work | No — iZinga imposes no method, script, or channel requirements |
| Control over hours | No — no fixed hours, targets, or minimum referral volumes |
| Forms part of the organisation | No — operates entirely outside iZinga's internal structure |
| 40+ hours/month average | Structurally impossible — a customer referral is a single-moment referral act |
| Economic dependence | Very low risk — customer referrals yield flat bounties; cannot substitute a wage |
| Tools of trade provided | Partial — referral code/link is a facilitation tool only |
| Exclusivity | No — Referral Partners may refer customers to competing platforms |

**Verdict (customer referral types):** Properly classified as independent contractors. The customer referral relationship is arguably the lightest-touch contractor relationship iZinga operates — lighter than the driver Ambassador relationship — because there is no requirement for the Referral Partner to be physically present, to assist with a process, or to stay engaged until an outcome is reached.

#### 1.2 Food Store Partner Referral Type

The food store partner referral involves the Referral Partner actively assisting a prospective store in setting up on iZinga's business platform (biz.izinga.co.za), including configuring menu categories, items, and item-level pricing. This is more analogous to the driver Ambassador model (in-person facilitation) and requires closer analysis.

| Factor (s200A LRA) | Food Store Referral Partner Position |
|---|---|
| Control over manner of work | No — iZinga does not prescribe how the Referral Partner onboards a store |
| Control over hours | No — no fixed hours or targets |
| Forms part of the organisation | No — operates independently; is not a member of iZinga's merchant acquisition team |
| 40+ hours/month average | Not structurally imposed — most onboardings are single-session |
| Economic dependence | Low — R250 per store is not a wage substitute |
| Tools of trade provided | Partial — biz.izinga.co.za platform access and referral code are facilitation tools |
| Exclusivity | No — Referral Partners are free to assist stores in listing on other platforms |

**Verdict (food store referral type):** Still properly classified as independent contractor on the current model. The in-person setup assistance is a facilitation act, not a controlled service delivery. iZinga does not supervise, schedule, or instruct the Referral Partner during the store setup. The commission structure (outcome-based, two-stage) further supports contractor classification — payment is contingent on results, not on hours of work.

**Risk to monitor (food store referral specifically):** If iZinga begins providing detailed step-by-step scripts, mandatory visit schedules, or area-based quotas for store partner referrals, those elements would push against the independent contractor classification and should be reviewed by Jason van der Merwe before introduction.

### 2. POPIA Position on Referred Third-Party Data

This Agreement raises a POPIA issue not present in the driver Ambassador ICA: the Referral Partner will, in the course of referral activity, provide iZinga with personal information about third parties — specifically the name and contact details of referred customers, and the business contact details and trading information of referred store partners. The Referral Partner is therefore a **source of personal information** in the meaning of section 18 of POPIA, and iZinga's collection of that information from the Referral Partner requires a lawful basis.

**Analysis — self-serve referral path (referred party acts themselves):**
- For referred customers who click a link and self-register: iZinga's lawful basis is likely **legitimate interest** (completing the referral and attributing the commission) combined with the referred person's subsequent **consent** when they register and accept iZinga's own privacy policy. iZinga must ensure its customer-facing Privacy Policy discloses that iZinga may receive referral introductions from third parties.
- For referred store partners who self-register on biz.izinga.co.za: similar position, with the store partner's own acceptance of iZinga's merchant terms and privacy notice providing the primary lawful basis once they engage with the platform.
- The Referral Partner must be explicitly prohibited from sharing more personal information than iZinga needs (name and contact details), and must warrant that they have not obtained the referred party's information unlawfully.

**Analysis — direct action path (Referral Partner acts on behalf of the referred party):**

This Agreement permits a second mode of operation — the Referral Partner may personally use the iZinga platform to place a food order, schedule a furniture delivery, or complete a store signup directly, acting on behalf of and with the authorisation of the referred customer or store owner. This mode creates a materially different and more acute POPIA exposure:

- Where a Referral Partner personally enters a third party's personal information into iZinga's platform (name, contact details, delivery address, banking details, business information, menu data), that Referral Partner is actively processing a third party's personal information. This is more than a simple referral introduction — it brings the referred party's personal information into iZinga's system under an account or record that the referred party never personally created or consented to.
- iZinga's lawful basis for processing that information cannot rely on the referred party's subsequent self-registration consent, because the referred party never self-registered. The sole lawful basis in this path is either **consent** (the referred party has expressly authorised the Referral Partner to act for them and to input their information) or **legitimate interest** (iZinga's interest in completing and attributing the referral, weighed against the referred party's privacy interests — a less secure basis where the referred party did not choose to engage with iZinga directly).
- The recommended position is that the **Referral Partner must obtain the referred party's express prior authorisation** before entering that party's personal information into iZinga's platform. This authorisation protects both iZinga (if a customer or store owner later disputes the account or order) and the Referral Partner (against a POPIA claim for unlawful processing). A warrant to this effect is included in clause 2.5 and clause 5.2.
- A store owner whose menu, pricing, and business details are entered by a third party without their knowledge has a cognisable complaint against both the Referral Partner and iZinga. This is a real risk for the store partner referral type in particular.

**Open question for Jason (new, see Part C item 12):** Does the direct-action path, where the Referral Partner enters a customer's personal information and executes a transaction on their behalf, require a more formal written authority (e.g. a signed mandate from the customer or store owner) before iZinga can safely process the resulting account or order? Or is a verbal authorisation — warranted by the Referral Partner in clause 2.5 — sufficient, with the risk sitting with the Referral Partner under the indemnity in clause 10.3? This requires Jason's view before the direct-action path goes live.

**This POPIA position requires Jason van der Merwe's confirmation before this Agreement is used** — the referred-third-party data flow (and particularly the direct-action path) is legally more complex than anything in the driver Ambassador ICA.

### 3. Estate Agency Affairs Act

Does not apply — Referral Partners are not involved in property transactions.

### 4. Consumer Protection Act

Section 17 (cooling-off) is not directly triggered by the referral partner relationship itself. Referred customers and store partners retain their own CPA rights under iZinga's customer-facing and merchant-facing terms.

### 5. Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act (FAIS)

Does not apply — Referral Partners are not providing financial advice or intermediary services.

### 2. SERVICES

2.1 The Referral Partner may perform one or more of the following referral services ("**Services**"), each as defined in Schedule 1:

(a) **Food Customer Referral** — referring individuals who become iZinga food delivery customers;

(b) **Furniture Customer Referral** — referring individuals who become iZinga furniture delivery customers;

(c) **Food Store Partner Referral** — referring and assisting local food retailers, supermarkets, and restaurants in registering and listing as store partners on iZinga's business platform (biz.izinga.co.za).

(a) **Referral link / code path** — the Referral Partner shares their unique Referral Code or referral link with a prospective customer or store owner, and that party independently clicks the link, registers on iZinga's platform, and completes the qualifying action themselves; or

(b) **Direct action path** — the Referral Partner personally uses iZinga's platform to perform the qualifying action on behalf of and with the prior express authorisation of the referred party, including placing a food order, scheduling a furniture delivery, or completing a store registration and menu setup on biz.izinga.co.za on that party's behalf.

(a) conclude any agreement on iZinga's behalf;

(b) make representations about iZinga's commission rates, fee structures, approval timelines, or earnings guarantees to any prospective customer or store partner that are not published on iZinga's official platforms;

(c) collect any fee or payment from a prospective customer or store partner in connection with iZinga registration or listing;

(d) take custody of, retain, photograph, or store copies of a referred party's identity documents, banking details, or other personal information outside of what is necessary to complete the action the referred party has expressly authorised the Referral Partner to perform on their behalf. The Referral Partner must not separately collect, photograph, or retain copies of any personal or financial information entered into the platform for or on behalf of a referred party;

(e) configure delivery vehicle pricing, platform fee rates, or delivery cost structures on behalf of iZinga or any referred store partner. Delivery and vehicle pricing is calculated automatically by iZinga's platform and is not within the Referral Partner's scope.

2.4 The Referral Partner acknowledges that iZinga retains sole discretion to approve or decline any referred customer account or store partner application and that referral does not guarantee approval or listing.

2.5 The Referral Partner must not make any misrepresentation to a prospective customer or store partner about the nature of their relationship with iZinga, earnings potential, delivery costs, or the registration or approval process.

2.6 **Direct action path — authorisation requirement:**

Where the Referral Partner uses the direct action path under clause 2.2(b), the Referral Partner:

(a) must obtain the express prior authorisation of the referred party before entering that party's personal information into iZinga's platform or performing any action on that party's behalf;

(b) warrants that such authorisation has been obtained before each use of the direct action path;

(c) must not enter payment or banking details of any referred party into iZinga's platform unless the referred party is present at the time of entry and has expressly directed the Referral Partner to do so;

(d) must inform the referred party that an account or order will be created or placed in their name on iZinga's platform as a result of the Referral Partner's actions on their behalf;

(e) must not use the direct action path to create accounts or execute transactions without the knowledge of the referred party.

iZinga is entitled to rely on the Referral Partner's warranties in this clause and will not independently verify that the referred party's authorisation was obtained. If a referred party disputes that they authorised the Referral Partner to act on their behalf, the Referral Partner indemnifies iZinga in accordance with clause 10.3.

### 3. ATTRIBUTION AND TRACKING

3.1 Each Referral Partner is assigned a unique referral identifier ("**Referral Code**") by iZinga. Attribution of a referred customer or store partner to a Referral Partner is determined solely by the Referral Code recorded in iZinga's system in connection with the qualifying action, as follows:

(a) **Referral link / code path** — for customer referrals, the Referral Code captured in the referred customer's registration record at the time of first registration on iZinga's platform; for store partner referrals, the Referral Code captured in the store partner's registration record on biz.izinga.co.za at the time of first registration.

(b) **Direct action path** — where the Referral Partner performs the qualifying action on behalf of the referred party using their own iZinga platform session, attribution is determined by the Referral Partner's authenticated session or account identifier recorded against the relevant order, account, or registration in iZinga's system at the time the action is completed.

3.2 iZinga's records of attribution are final and binding for the purpose of calculating Referral Partner commission, absent manifest error.

3.3 The Referral Partner must not manipulate, circumvent, or attempt to duplicate the attribution tracking system or create multiple referral identifiers for the same individual or store.

3.4 A referred customer or store partner may only be attributed to one Referral Partner. Where a conflict arises between two Referral Partners claiming attribution for the same referred party, iZinga will resolve the conflict by reference to the first-in-time attribution event recorded in iZinga's records, and iZinga's determination is final.

> **Implementation note (open item — see Part C, items 1 and 12):** The attribution mechanism must support two technically distinct flows. For the referral link / code path, the Referral Code must be embedded in the registration or checkout flow when a referred party self-acts. For the direct action path, the platform must be able to record "this action was performed by Referral Partner X on behalf of a third party" at the session or transaction level, distinct from a standard customer-initiated action. This is a larger technical lift than a simple referral code — it may require a distinct "acting on behalf of" mode in the platform, and may require the platform to capture evidence of the referred party's authorisation at the point of action. Engineering must confirm the implementation before this Agreement is used. The legal attribution mechanic described in clause 3.1 is the intended position — the implementation must reflect it exactly.

5.6 The Referral Partner must not collect, store, retain, or further process any personal information about referred parties beyond what is necessary to perform the specific referral action, and must not retain any copies of personal information entered into iZinga's platform on a referred party's behalf.

### Schedule 1 — Clause 2: Furniture Customer Referral

2.1 The 5% commission is earned once per referred customer, on the date the referred customer's first furniture delivery order is confirmed as completed and paid in iZinga's system.

2.2 If a referred customer registers on iZinga's platform but never completes a furniture delivery, no commission is payable. No commission is payable on any delivery after the first, regardless of how many further deliveries that customer places.

2.3 Two defined terms govern the furniture referral commission calculation. They must not be conflated — they refer to different amounts and are used for different purposes within the same transaction.

"**Base Delivery Fee**" means the core delivery charge set by iZinga for a specific furniture delivery, before the addition of any service fee. This is the amount on which the **driver's payout** is calculated.

"**Total Delivery Charge**" means the Base Delivery Fee plus iZinga's 6.5% service fee calculated on that Base Delivery Fee. This is the **total amount the customer pays** for the delivery and is the amount on which the **Referral Partner's 5% commission** is calculated.

For the avoidance of doubt:

(a) The driver's payout is 90% of the **Base Delivery Fee** only. The driver's share does not include any portion of the 6.5% service fee.

(b) The Referral Partner's commission is 5% of the **Total Delivery Charge** (Base Delivery Fee plus the 6.5% service fee). The Referral Partner's commission is therefore calculated on a larger base than the driver's payout.

Worked example (Base Delivery Fee of R500):

| Component | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Base Delivery Fee | — | R500.00 |
| 6.5% service fee | R500.00 × 6.5% | R32.50 |
| Total Delivery Charge (customer pays) | R500.00 + R32.50 | R532.50 |
| Driver payout | 90% × R500.00 | R450.00 |
| Yoco processing fee | 2.9% × R532.50 | R15.44 |
| iZinga gross margin before referral commission | R532.50 − R450.00 | R82.50 |
| Referral Partner commission (once-off, on first delivery only) | 5% × R532.50 | R26.63 |
| iZinga true net margin after driver, Yoco, and referral commission | R532.50 − R450.00 − R15.44 − R26.63 | R40.43 |

VAT, if charged to the customer as a separately itemised line on their invoice or receipt, is excluded from the Total Delivery Charge base for the purpose of calculating Referral Partner commission. Any insurance surcharge charged as a separately itemised line is also excluded. If VAT or insurance is bundled into the delivery charge without separate itemisation, the full bundled amount is treated as the Total Delivery Charge base unless iZinga notifies the Referral Partner otherwise in writing.

> **Platform economics context (confirmed and corrected 14 July 2026 — for internal reference, not part of the agreement text):** For furniture deliveries, the Total Delivery Charge is iZinga's entire revenue line — there is no separate goods-value commission. The driver's payout is anchored to the Base Delivery Fee (90% of R500 = R450 in the example); iZinga's gross margin is the remainder of the Total Delivery Charge (R532.50 − R450.00 = R82.50, equivalent to 10% of the Base Delivery Fee plus 100% of the 6.5% service fee). The Referral Partner's 5%-of-Total-Delivery-Charge commission (R26.63 in the example) consumes approximately **32% of iZinga's gross margin** (R26.63 / R82.50). Note: an earlier internal calculation stated this figure as 50% — that was a calculation error caused by incorrectly applying both the driver's split and the referral commission to the same base amount (R500), rather than to their respective correct bases (R500 for the driver, R532.50 for the Referral Partner). The corrected figure is 32%. See Part C item 5 for the required commercial sign-off.
