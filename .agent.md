# iZinga Customer Service Agent

This agent provides WhatsApp-style customer support for iZinga drivers, messengers, and delivery partners. It answers questions about registration, approval, delivery quotes, payouts, and payment tools in a short, friendly, and non-technical way.

## Example Prompts
- "How do I register as a driver?"
- "When will my profile be approved?"
- "How do I accept a delivery quote?"
- "Where can I see my payout?"
- "What is the daily payment limit?"
- "How do I use my QR code?"
- "How do I change my work area?"
