1. Nature of Relationship

1.1 The Ambassador is engaged by iZinga as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship, partnership, joint venture, or any other relationship other than that of principal and independent contractor.

1.2 The Ambassador is not an employee of iZinga for any purpose, including the Labour Relations Act 66 of 1995, the Basic Conditions of Employment Act 75 of 1997, the Unemployment Insurance Act 63 of 2001, and the Income Tax Act 58 of 1962.

1.3 iZinga does not control the manner, method, time, place, or means by which the Ambassador performs the Services. The Ambassador is free to determine their own working hours, methods, and geographic focus.

1.4 This Agreement does not create exclusivity. The Ambassador may engage in other business activities, including referral activities for third parties.

1.5 The Ambassador is under no obligation to perform any minimum quantity of services under this Agreement. iZinga is under no obligation to provide any minimum volume of referral opportunities to the Ambassador. Neither Party makes any guarantee as to the volume of activity that will arise under this Agreement.

2. Services

2.1 The Ambassador agrees to promote iZinga's driver and messenger onboarding programme and to introduce prospective delivery drivers and messengers ("Referred Drivers") to iZinga's registration platform.

2.2 The Ambassador's services are limited to referral and promotion. The Ambassador has no authority to: (a) conclude any agreement on iZinga's behalf; (b) make representations about iZinga's commission rates, approval timelines, or earnings guarantees not published on iZinga's official platforms; (c) collect any fee, payment, or documentation from a prospective driver on iZinga's behalf.

2.3 The Ambassador acknowledges that iZinga retains sole discretion to approve or decline any Referred Driver and that referral does not guarantee approval.

2.4 The Ambassador must not make any misrepresentation to a prospective driver about the nature of the driver's relationship with iZinga, earnings potential, or the approval process.

3. Attribution and Tracking

3.1 Each Ambassador is assigned a unique referral identifier ("Ambassador ID") by iZinga. Attribution is determined solely by the Ambassador ID captured in the Referred Driver's registration record at the time of registration.

3.2 iZinga's records of attribution are final and binding for the purpose of calculating Ambassador commission, absent manifest error.

3.3 The Ambassador must not manipulate, circumvent, or attempt to duplicate the attribution tracking system.

4. Commission

4.1 iZinga will pay the Ambassador a commission of R70.00 (seventy rand) per Referred Driver per payout run in which that Referred Driver processes at least one completed delivery.

4.2 Commission is paid as part of iZinga's automated daily payout run at or around 17:00 each business day, to the bank account registered by the Ambassador on the iZinga onboarding platform.

4.3 Commission is earned only once a Referred Driver has been: (a) approved by iZinga following completion of all required onboarding steps; and (b) included in a payout run in which they have processed at least one completed and paid delivery.

4.4 No commission is payable for: (a) a Referred Driver who registers but is not approved; (b) a Referred Driver who processes no deliveries in a given payout run; (c) a Referred Driver whose account is suspended at the time of the payout run.

4.5 iZinga reserves the right to adjust the commission rate on 30 calendar days' written notice. Continued use of the Ambassador platform after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised rate.

4.6 The Ambassador is responsible for all taxes, levies, and statutory contributions applicable to commission received, including provisional income tax. iZinga will not deduct PAYE, UIF, or SDL from commission payments.

5. Intellectual Property

5.1 All referral materials, QR codes, referral links, promotional copy, and digital assets created by iZinga and provided to the Ambassador remain the exclusive intellectual property of iZinga.

5.2 Any referral materials created by the Ambassador that incorporate iZinga's name, logo, or trade marks are hereby assigned to iZinga with effect from the date of creation, to the fullest extent permitted by the Copyright Act 98 of 1978.

5.3 The Ambassador may only use iZinga's brand assets in materials that are accurate, not misleading, and comply with iZinga's brand guidelines.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 The Ambassador must keep confidential all non-public information about iZinga's business, commission rates, driver approval criteria, platform technology, and partner relationships. This obligation survives termination for 2 years.

8. Termination

8.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement on 30 calendar days' written notice.

8.2 iZinga may terminate immediately and without notice if the Ambassador: (a) makes a misrepresentation to a prospective driver; (b) charges a prospective driver any fee; (c) manipulates the attribution tracking system; (d) misuses iZinga's brand assets; (e) brings iZinga into disrepute; or (f) engages in fraudulent conduct.

8.3 On termination: the Ambassador's dashboard access is suspended; Ambassador-Created Materials must be removed within 7 days; commission accrued before termination is paid in the next scheduled payout run; no further commission accrues after the termination date.

9. Liability

9.1 iZinga's total liability to the Ambassador is limited to the commission earned but unpaid at the date of the relevant claim. iZinga is not liable for any loss of anticipated commission, indirect loss, or consequential loss.

9.2 The Ambassador indemnifies iZinga against any loss, claim, penalty, or expense arising from any misrepresentation made to a prospective driver, the Ambassador's failure to comply with applicable law, or the Ambassador's tax obligations.

10. Dispute Resolution

10.1 The Parties will attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation within 14 calendar days of written notice. If unresolved, either Party may refer the dispute to expedited arbitration administered by the Arbitration Foundation of Southern Africa (AFSA), with a single arbitrator, conducted in English, in Johannesburg or Durban (at iZinga's election). The arbitrator's award is final and binding.

11.1 Governing law: The laws of the Republic of South Africa.

11.2 Jurisdiction: High Court of South Africa, Gauteng Division (Johannesburg).

11.3 Entire agreement: This Agreement supersedes all prior representations or understandings. No oral variation is binding.

11.4 Amendment: iZinga may amend this Agreement on 30 calendar days' written notice. Continued use of the Ambassador platform after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

11.5 Electronic acceptance: This Agreement is concluded electronically when the Ambassador clicks the acceptance button. Electronic acceptance constitutes a valid and binding signature under the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002.
