Privacy Policy

How iZinga collects, uses, and protects your data

🔐 POPIA Compliance

iZinga is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), Act 4 of 2013 (South Africa).

1. What Data We Collect
  • Full name and contact details (phone number, email address)
  • Identity document or passport number (for verification)
  • Bank account or mobile wallet details (for payouts)
  • Vehicle registration and licence information (for drivers)
  • Business registration details (for shop owners)
  • Delivery address and location data (to fulfil orders)
2. How We Use Your Data

Your personal information is collected and stored exclusively on the iZinga platform and is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To verify your identity and approve your account
  • To process and fulfil delivery orders
  • To calculate and process payouts to drivers and store owners
  • To communicate important updates about your account or orders
  • To comply with South African legal and regulatory requirements
3. Who Can Access Your Data

Your personal data is not shared with any third party. Access is strictly limited to:

  • iZinga administrators — for account verification, support, and compliance purposes
  • Assigned drivers — only the minimum information required (e.g. delivery address, customer name) to successfully complete a delivery
4. Data Storage & Security
  • All data is stored securely on the iZinga platform with encryption and access controls
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information
  • Data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes listed above or as required by law
5. Your Rights Under POPIA
  • The right to access the personal information we hold about you
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate information
  • The right to request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • The right to object to the processing of your personal information
6. Contact Our Information Officer

For any privacy-related requests or complaints, please contact us via the details on the Contact Us page on the iZinga website.

⚠️ Notice: By using the iZinga platform you consent to the collection and use of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy, in accordance with POPIA.
