How Does It Work? Onboarding Steps

Follow these steps to start receiving and completing delivery quotes on iZinga.

1
Sign up
Create your profile with your phone number and bank details.
2
Upload documents
Upload your driver's license, PDP, vehicle documents, and vehicle photos.
3
Profile review and criminal check
Your profile will go through review and criminal background checks.
4
Receive and accept trip quotation via WhatsApp
A delivery order is sent on WhatsApp. Review and accept only quotes you can fulfill.
5
Pickup the parcel
Collect the parcel from the pickup location shown on the quote.
6
Complete drop-off
Deliver to the drop-off location shown on the quote.
7
Close the quote after delivery
After drop-off, close the quote to receive funds into your iZinga wallet.
8
Scan QR code for extra tips
If a customer wants to tip you, present your QR code and the tip goes to your wallet.
9
Request payout
Your earnings are batched daily and paid to your bank account or e-wallet the next business day.
