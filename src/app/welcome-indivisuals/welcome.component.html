Get Paid Seamlessly with iZinga!

Want an easy and secure way to receive payments or tips? Look no further! Izinga offers a convenient platform for you to get paid directly into your bank account or cellphone number and withdraw your money from any ATM.

Why Choose iZinga?

  • 💵 Instant Payments: Receive payments or tips instantly into your bank account or cellphone number.
  • 🏧 Easy Withdrawals: Withdraw your money from any ATM whenever you need it.
  • 🔒 Secure Transactions: Enjoy safe and secure payment processing.
  • Payment Limit: Payments to a cellphone number have a daily withdrawal limit of R3000. If your balance exceeds R3000, the remaining amount will be paid out on the next payout day.
  • 👌 Convenient for Customers: Easy for customers to tip you without the hassle of cash.

Steps to Register:

  • 1. Create Your Account:sign up with your phone number, name and your prefered bank account.
  • 2. Download Your Unique QR Code: After registration, download your personalized QR code.
  • 3. Start Receiving Payments: Share your QR code with your customers, who can scan it to tip you instantly!
