Why Join iZinga?

Boost your business with a digital storefront and secure payments.

  • 📈 Grow Your Business: List your shop on iZinga and attract more customers to your products.
  • 🛒 Dedicated Store Page: Get your own store page where customers can easily browse and purchase your products.
  • 💵 Instant Payments: Receive your earnings directly into your bank account or cellphone number as soon as the sale is made.
  • 🏧 Easy Withdrawals: Withdraw your money from any ATM whenever you need it, without any hassle.
  • 🔒 Secure Transactions: Enjoy peace of mind with safe and secure payment processing for every order and tip you receive.
  • 💸 Daily Payouts: Get daily payouts directly to your bank account or e-wallet, ensuring you have quick access to your earnings.
  • 📋 Withdrawal Limits: Payments to a cellphone number have a daily withdrawal limit of R3000. Any amount exceeding R3000 will be carried over and paid out on the next payout day.
  • 👌 Convenient Tipping: Let your customers easily tip you using your unique QR code, with funds transferred directly to your account.

How to Get Started

Follow these simple steps to set up your store and start earning.

  • 1. Sign Up: Create your account with your phone number, full name, and preferred bank account or e-wallet.
  • 2. Set Up Your Store: List your shop on iZinga, customize your store page, and add your products to start selling.
  • 3. Download Your QR Code: Receive your personalized QR code to make tipping easy and seamless for your customers.
  • 4. Start Selling: Watch your sales grow as customers discover your shop and place orders directly through iZinga!
