1. Nature of Relationship

1.1 The Referral Partner is engaged by iZinga as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship, partnership, joint venture, or any other relationship other than that of principal and independent contractor.

1.2 The Referral Partner is not an employee of iZinga for any purpose, including the Labour Relations Act 66 of 1995, the Basic Conditions of Employment Act 75 of 1997, the Unemployment Insurance Act 63 of 2001, and the Income Tax Act 58 of 1962.

1.3 iZinga does not control the manner, method, time, place, or means by which the Referral Partner performs the Services. The Referral Partner is free to determine their own working hours, methods, and geographic focus.

1.4 This Agreement does not create exclusivity. The Referral Partner may refer customers or stores to other platforms.

2. Services

2.1 The Referral Partner agrees to promote iZinga's food ordering, furniture delivery, and merchant onboarding programmes and to introduce prospective customers and store owners to iZinga's platforms.

2.2 The Referral Partner has no authority to: (a) conclude any agreement on iZinga's behalf; (b) make representations about iZinga's pricing, approval timelines, or earnings guarantees not published on iZinga's official platforms; (c) collect any fee, payment, or documentation from a referred party on iZinga's behalf.

3. Attribution and Tracking

3.1 Each Referral Partner is assigned a unique referral code by iZinga. Attribution is determined solely by the referral code captured in the referred party's registration record at the time of registration.

3.2 iZinga's records of attribution are final and binding for commission calculations, absent manifest error.

4. Commission

4.1 Commission rates are as set out in Schedule 1 above.

4.2 Commission is paid as part of iZinga's automated daily payout run, to the bank account registered by the Referral Partner on the iZinga platform.

4.3 The Referral Partner is responsible for all taxes, levies, and statutory contributions applicable to commission received. iZinga will not deduct PAYE, UIF, or SDL from commission payments.

4.4 iZinga reserves the right to adjust commission rates on 30 calendar days' written notice.

5. Data Processing and POPIA

5.1 By accepting this Agreement, the Referral Partner consents to iZinga processing their personal information (name, contact details, bank account details, and referral records) for the purposes of administering this Agreement and processing commission payouts.

5.2 The Referral Partner must not share more personal information about referred parties than iZinga requires (name and contact details), and warrants that any such information was obtained lawfully.

5.3 Processing is on the dual lawful basis of contractual necessity and consent. Retention period: 5 years from termination of this Agreement.

6. Termination

6.1 Either party may terminate on 30 calendar days' written notice.

6.2 iZinga may terminate immediately if the Referral Partner makes misrepresentations, manipulates the attribution system, or engages in fraudulent conduct.

7. Electronic Acceptance (clause 12.5)

7.1 This Agreement is concluded electronically when the Referral Partner clicks the acceptance button below. Electronic acceptance constitutes a valid and binding signature under the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002. iZinga records the acceptance timestamp and agreement version as part of the Referral Partner's profile.

8. General

8.1 Governing law: The laws of the Republic of South Africa.

8.2 Jurisdiction: High Court of South Africa, Gauteng Division (Johannesburg).

8.3 Entire agreement: This Agreement supersedes all prior representations or understandings.
