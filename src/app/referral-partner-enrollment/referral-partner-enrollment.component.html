This Referral Partner Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between:

iZinga, a product brand operated by Curiousoft (Pty) Ltd, registration number 2016/429327/07, a private company incorporated in the Republic of South Africa, with its registered address at 68 Jacaranda Avenue, Olivedale, Randburg, 2188 ("iZinga" or "the Company");

and the person who has accepted this Agreement electronically through iZinga's referral enrolment process ("the Referral Partner").

iZinga and the Referral Partner are referred to individually as "a Party" and collectively as "the Parties."

1. Nature of Relationship

1.1 The Referral Partner is engaged by iZinga as an independent contractor for the limited purpose of referring prospective customers and store partners to iZinga's platforms. Nothing in this Agreement creates or shall be construed to create an employment relationship, a partnership, a joint venture, a mandate to bind iZinga contractually, or any other relationship between the Parties other than that of principal and independent contractor.

1.2 The Referral Partner is not an employee of iZinga for any purpose, including but not limited to the Labour Relations Act 66 of 1995, the Basic Conditions of Employment Act 75 of 1997, the Unemployment Insurance Act 63 of 2001, and the Income Tax Act 58 of 1962.

1.3 iZinga does not control the manner, method, time, place, or means by which the Referral Partner performs the Services. The Referral Partner is free to determine their own working hours, referral methods, and geographic focus without direction from iZinga.

1.4 This Agreement does not create exclusivity in favour of either Party. The Referral Partner may engage in referral activities for third parties, including iZinga's competitors, provided such activities do not constitute a breach of clause 8 of this Agreement.

2. Services

2.1 The Referral Partner may perform one or more of the following referral services ("Services"), each as defined in Schedule 1:

(a) Food Customer Referral — referring individuals who become iZinga food delivery customers;

(b) Furniture Customer Referral — referring individuals who become iZinga furniture delivery customers;

(c) Food Store Partner Referral — referring and assisting local food retailers, supermarkets, and restaurants in registering and listing as store partners on iZinga's business platform (biz.izinga.co.za).

The commission payable for each referral type, and the conditions that must be satisfied for commission to be earned, are set out in Schedule 1 to this Agreement.

2.2 The Referral Partner may perform the Services in either or both of the following ways:

(a) Referral link / code path — the Referral Partner shares their unique Referral Code or referral link with a prospective customer or store owner, and that party independently clicks the link, registers on iZinga's platform, and completes the qualifying action themselves; or

(b) Direct action path — the Referral Partner personally uses iZinga's platform to perform the qualifying action on behalf of and with the prior express authorisation of the referred party, including placing a food order, scheduling a furniture delivery, or completing a store registration and menu setup on biz.izinga.co.za on that party's behalf.

The direct action path is subject to the authorisation requirements in clause 2.6. Both paths are subject to the prohibitions in clause 2.3.

2.3 The Referral Partner has no authority to:

(a) conclude any agreement on iZinga's behalf;

(b) make representations about iZinga's commission rates, fee structures, approval timelines, or earnings guarantees to any prospective customer or store partner that are not published on iZinga's official platforms;

(c) collect any fee or payment from a prospective customer or store partner in connection with iZinga registration or listing;

(d) take custody of, retain, photograph, or store copies of a referred party's identity documents, banking details, or other personal information outside of what is necessary to complete the action the referred party has expressly authorised the Referral Partner to perform on their behalf. The Referral Partner must not separately collect, photograph, or retain copies of any personal or financial information entered into the platform for or on behalf of a referred party;

(e) configure delivery vehicle pricing, platform fee rates, or delivery cost structures on behalf of iZinga or any referred store partner. Delivery and vehicle pricing is calculated automatically by iZinga's platform and is not within the Referral Partner's scope.

2.4 The Referral Partner acknowledges that iZinga retains sole discretion to approve or decline any referred customer account or store partner application and that referral does not guarantee approval or listing.

2.5 The Referral Partner must not make any misrepresentation to a prospective customer or store partner about the nature of their relationship with iZinga, earnings potential, delivery costs, or the registration or approval process.

2.6 Direct action path — authorisation requirement:

Where the Referral Partner uses the direct action path under clause 2.2(b), the Referral Partner:

(a) must obtain the express prior authorisation of the referred party before entering that party's personal information into iZinga's platform or performing any action on that party's behalf;

(b) warrants that such authorisation has been obtained before each use of the direct action path;

(c) must not enter payment or banking details of any referred party into iZinga's platform unless the referred party is present at the time of entry and has expressly directed the Referral Partner to do so;

(d) must inform the referred party that an account or order will be created or placed in their name on iZinga's platform as a result of the Referral Partner's actions on their behalf;

(e) must not use the direct action path to create accounts or execute transactions without the knowledge of the referred party.

iZinga is entitled to rely on the Referral Partner's warranties in this clause and will not independently verify that the referred party's authorisation was obtained. If a referred party disputes that they authorised the Referral Partner to act on their behalf, the Referral Partner indemnifies iZinga in accordance with clause 10.3.

3. Attribution and Tracking

3.1 Each Referral Partner is assigned a unique referral identifier ("Referral Code") by iZinga. Attribution of a referred customer or store partner to a Referral Partner is determined solely by the Referral Code recorded in iZinga's system in connection with the qualifying action, as follows:

(a) Referral link / code path — for customer referrals, the Referral Code captured in the referred customer's registration record at the time of first registration on iZinga's platform; for store partner referrals, the Referral Code captured in the store partner's registration record on biz.izinga.co.za at the time of first registration.

(b) Direct action path — where the Referral Partner performs the qualifying action on behalf of the referred party using their own iZinga platform session, attribution is determined by the Referral Partner's authenticated session or account identifier recorded against the relevant order, account, or registration in iZinga's system at the time the action is completed.

3.2 iZinga's records of attribution are final and binding for the purpose of calculating Referral Partner commission, absent manifest error.

3.3 The Referral Partner must not manipulate, circumvent, or attempt to duplicate the attribution tracking system or create multiple referral identifiers for the same individual or store.

3.4 A referred customer or store partner may only be attributed to one Referral Partner. Where a conflict arises between two Referral Partners claiming attribution for the same referred party, iZinga will resolve the conflict by reference to the first-in-time attribution event recorded in iZinga's records, and iZinga's determination is final.

4. Commission

4.1 iZinga will pay the Referral Partner commission in accordance with Schedule 1.

4.2 Commission is calculated and paid as part of iZinga's standard payout run. Payment is made to the bank account registered by the Referral Partner on iZinga's referral enrolment platform.

4.3 No commission is payable for:

(a) a referred customer who registers on iZinga's platform but does not complete the qualifying transaction specified in Schedule 1;

(b) a referred store partner whose application is not approved and listed on iZinga's platform;

(c) any referral that cannot be attributed to the Referral Partner through the Referral Code mechanism described in clause 3.

4.4 iZinga reserves the right to adjust commission rates on 30 (thirty) calendar days' written notice to the Referral Partner. Continued use of the Referral Partner platform after the effective date of a rate change constitutes acceptance of the revised rates.

4.5 The Referral Partner is responsible for all taxes, levies, and statutory contributions applicable to commission received under this Agreement, including provisional income tax. iZinga will not deduct PAYE, UIF, or SDL from commission payments. The Referral Partner indemnifies iZinga against any SARS assessment, penalty, or interest arising from the Referral Partner's failure to comply with their own tax obligations.

4.6 If the Referral Partner is registered for VAT, the Referral Partner must notify iZinga in writing and must issue a valid tax invoice for each commission payment. iZinga's commission figures in such cases will be construed as VAT-exclusive unless the Referral Partner's VAT status has been confirmed and a valid tax invoice issued.

5. Referred Third-Party Personal Information and POPIA

5.1 In the course of performing the Services, the Referral Partner may cause personal information about the individuals or businesses being referred ("Referred Party Information") to come into iZinga's possession. This may occur in two ways:

(a) Referral link / code path — the Referral Partner shares contact details of a prospective customer or store owner with iZinga in advance of or alongside a referral introduction; or

(b) Direct action path — the Referral Partner personally enters the referred party's personal information (including name, contact details, delivery address, business details, banking details, and menu data) into iZinga's platform while acting on behalf of the referred party under clause 2.2(b).

The POPIA obligations in this clause apply to both paths, with additional obligations for the direct action path as set out in clause 5.3.

5.2 By accepting this Agreement, the Referral Partner warrants that:

(a) any Referred Party Information provided to or entered into iZinga's platform has been obtained lawfully and with the referred party's knowledge;

(b) the Referral Partner will not provide iZinga with more personal information about a referred party than is necessary for the referral type being performed;

(c) where the Referral Partner is using the referral link / code path, the Referral Partner will not represent to a referred party that they have been registered with iZinga or that iZinga will contact them without first informing the referred party that their details will be shared with iZinga for the purpose of a referral introduction;

(d) the Referral Partner understands that iZinga will process Referred Party Information only to the extent necessary for the purposes set out in this Agreement and iZinga's Privacy Policy.

5.3 Direct action path — additional POPIA obligations:

Where the Referral Partner uses the direct action path under clause 2.2(b), the Referral Partner additionally warrants that:

(a) the referred party has expressly authorised the Referral Partner to enter their personal information into iZinga's platform and to perform the relevant action on their behalf, as required by clause 2.6;

(b) the referred party has been informed that an iZinga account or order record will be created or placed in their name as a result of the Referral Partner's actions;

(c) the referred party has been informed that iZinga will contact them in connection with that account or order, and has not objected to receiving such contact;

(d) to the extent the Referral Partner enters the referred party's banking or payment details into iZinga's platform, the referred party was present at the time and directed the Referral Partner to enter those details.

5.4 iZinga's lawful basis by path:

(a) For the referral link / code path: iZinga processes Referred Party Information on the lawful basis of legitimate interest (verifying and attributing the referral) until the referred party self-registers on iZinga's platform and accepts iZinga's Privacy Policy, at which point iZinga's further processing is governed by that Privacy Policy.

(b) For the direct action path: iZinga processes Referred Party Information on the basis of consent as warranted by the Referral Partner under clauses 2.6 and 5.3. Because iZinga cannot independently verify that the referred party's authorisation was obtained, iZinga's ability to rely on this lawful basis is contingent on the accuracy of the Referral Partner's warranties. If a referred party disputes that they authorised the Referral Partner to act on their behalf, iZinga reserves the right to delete or restrict processing of that party's account or order data pending resolution.

5.5 iZinga will not use Referred Party Information for any purpose unrelated to the referral it was provided for. iZinga will delete or anonymise Referred Party Information for a referred party under the referral link / code path who does not complete registration within 90 (ninety) days of the referral being received.

5.6 The Referral Partner must not collect, store, retain, or further process any personal information about referred parties beyond what is necessary to perform the specific referral action, and must not retain any copies of personal information entered into iZinga's platform on a referred party's behalf.

6. Referral Partner's Own Personal Information and POPIA Consent

6.1 By accepting this Agreement, the Referral Partner consents to iZinga processing the following personal information for the purposes set out below:

Category of Personal Information Purpose

Bank account details Processing commission payouts
Referral Code and referral attribution records Calculating and auditing commission entitlements

6.2 iZinga processes the Referral Partner's personal information on the dual lawful basis of:

(a) contractual necessity — processing is required to perform obligations under this Agreement; and

(b) consent — the Referral Partner expressly consents to the processing described in clause 6.1.

6.3 iZinga will retain the Referral Partner's personal information for a period of 5 (five) years from the date of termination of this Agreement, after which it will be deleted or anonymised, unless retention for a longer period is required by law.

6.4 The Referral Partner's personal information will not be sold or disclosed to third parties except:

(a) to iZinga's payment service providers for the purpose of processing commission payments;

(b) as required by law or a lawful order of a court or competent authority.

6.5 The Referral Partner has the right to:

(a) request access to their personal information held by iZinga;

(b) request correction of inaccurate personal information;

(c) request deletion of personal information (subject to iZinga's retention obligations);

(d) object to processing;

(e) lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

6.6 Requests under clause 6.5 may be directed to iZinga's Information Officer via the contact details published on iZinga's website.

6.7 This clause constitutes iZinga's POPIA disclosure for the purposes of section 18 of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013.

7. Intellectual Property

7.1 Any referral materials, marketing content, digital assets, referral codes, referral links, promotional copy, or other materials created by iZinga and provided to the Referral Partner remain the exclusive intellectual property of iZinga.

7.2 Any materials created by the Referral Partner that incorporate iZinga's name, logo, trade marks, or brand elements are hereby assigned to iZinga with effect from the date of creation, to the fullest extent permitted by the Copyright Act 98 of 1978. The Referral Partner waives any moral rights in such materials to the extent permitted by law.

7.3 The Referral Partner may only use iZinga's brand assets in materials that:

(a) are accurate and not misleading;

(b) do not make representations iZinga has not authorised;

(c) comply with iZinga's brand guidelines as published from time to time.

7.4 iZinga may require the Referral Partner to remove or modify any referral materials that do not comply with clause 7.3 within 48 hours of written notice.

8. Confidentiality

8.1 The Referral Partner must keep confidential all non-public information about iZinga's business, commission rates, store partner approval criteria, platform technology, and partner relationships that comes to the Referral Partner's knowledge in the course of performing the Services.

8.2 The confidentiality obligation in clause 8.1 survives termination of this Agreement for a period of 2 (two) years.

9. Termination

9.1 Notice termination: Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 (thirty) calendar days' written notice to the other Party.

9.2 Immediate termination by iZinga: iZinga may terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice if the Referral Partner:

(a) makes a misrepresentation to a prospective customer or store partner;

(b) charges or attempts to charge a prospective customer or store partner any fee in connection with iZinga registration;

(c) collects or retains personal information about a referred party in contravention of clause 5.4;

(d) manipulates or circumvents the Referral Code attribution tracking system;

(e) uses iZinga's brand assets in a manner that violates clause 7.3;

(f) brings iZinga into disrepute;

(g) is found to have engaged in fraudulent conduct.

9.3 Effect of termination — general provisions:

(a) The Referral Partner's access to iZinga's referral partner portal will be suspended on the effective date of termination.

(b) All iZinga-branded referral materials must be removed from the Referral Partner's channels within 7 (seven) calendar days of the termination date.

(c) Commission accrued and payable for qualifying transactions completed before the termination date will be paid in the next scheduled payout run after the effective date of termination.

(d) No new referral attributions may be recorded after the termination date.

10. Liability

10.1 iZinga's total liability to the Referral Partner under or in connection with this Agreement is limited to the commission earned but unpaid at the date of the relevant claim.

10.2 iZinga is not liable for any loss of anticipated commission, indirect loss, loss of profits, or consequential loss arising from the termination of this Agreement, any change to the commission structure, or any decision by iZinga to decline a referred customer or store partner application.

10.3 The Referral Partner indemnifies iZinga against any loss, claim, penalty, or expense arising from:

(a) any misrepresentation made by the Referral Partner to a prospective customer or store partner;

(b) the Referral Partner's failure to comply with applicable law, including POPIA, in the performance of the Services;

(c) the Referral Partner's failure to comply with the personal information obligations in clause 5;

(d) the Referral Partner's failure to obtain the referred party's express prior authorisation before using the direct action path under clause 2.6, including any claim by a referred party that they did not authorise the Referral Partner to create an account, place an order, or enter their personal information on their behalf;

(e) the Referral Partner's tax obligations under clause 4.5.

11. Dispute Resolution

11.1 The Parties will attempt to resolve any dispute arising from this Agreement through good-faith negotiation within 14 (fourteen) calendar days of written notice of the dispute.

11.2 If the dispute is not resolved through negotiation, either Party may refer the dispute to expedited arbitration administered by the Arbitration Foundation of Southern Africa (AFSA) under AFSA's rules, with a single arbitrator, conducted in English, in Johannesburg or Durban (at iZinga's election).

11.3 The arbitrator's award is final and binding and may be made an order of the High Court of South Africa.

11.4 Nothing in this clause prevents either Party from approaching a court of competent jurisdiction for urgent relief.

11.5 Nothing in this Agreement limits any rights the Referral Partner may have under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 that cannot be contractually excluded.

Schedule 1 — Clause 1: Food Customer Referral

1.1 The R15.00 commission is earned once per referred customer, on the date the referred customer's first food order is confirmed as completed and paid in iZinga's system.

1.2 If a referred customer registers on iZinga's platform but never completes a food order, no commission is payable.

1.3 Only one Referral Partner may earn commission in respect of any one referred food customer. Attribution is determined under clause 3 of this Agreement.

Schedule 1 — Clause 2: Furniture Customer Referral

2.1 The 5% commission is earned once per referred customer, on the date the referred customer's first furniture delivery order is confirmed as completed and paid in iZinga's system.

2.2 If a referred customer registers on iZinga's platform but never completes a furniture delivery, no commission is payable. No commission is payable on any delivery after the first, regardless of how many further deliveries that customer places.

2.3 Two defined terms govern the furniture referral commission calculation. They must not be conflated — they refer to different amounts and are used for different purposes within the same transaction.

"Base Delivery Fee" means the core delivery charge set by iZinga for a specific furniture delivery, before the addition of any service fee. This is the amount on which the driver's payout is calculated.

"Total Delivery Charge" means the Base Delivery Fee plus iZinga's 6.5% service fee calculated on that Base Delivery Fee. This is the total amount the customer pays for the delivery and is the amount on which the Referral Partner's 5% commission is calculated.

For the avoidance of doubt:

(a) The driver's payout is 90% of the Base Delivery Fee only. The driver's share does not include any portion of the 6.5% service fee.

(b) The Referral Partner's commission is 5% of the Total Delivery Charge (Base Delivery Fee plus the 6.5% service fee). The Referral Partner's commission is therefore calculated on a larger base than the driver's payout.

Worked example (Base Delivery Fee of R500):

Component Calculation Amount
Base Delivery Fee—R500.00
6.5% service feeR500.00 × 6.5%R32.50
Total Delivery Charge (customer pays)R500.00 + R32.50R532.50
Driver payout90% × R500.00R450.00
Yoco processing fee2.9% × R532.50R15.44
iZinga gross margin before referral commissionR532.50 − R450.00R82.50
Referral Partner commission (once-off, on first delivery only)5% × R532.50R26.63
iZinga true net margin after driver, Yoco, and referral commissionR532.50 − R450.00 − R15.44 − R26.63R40.43

VAT, if charged to the customer as a separately itemised line on their invoice or receipt, is excluded from the Total Delivery Charge base for the purpose of calculating Referral Partner commission. Any insurance surcharge charged as a separately itemised line is also excluded. If VAT or insurance is bundled into the delivery charge without separate itemisation, the full bundled amount is treated as the Total Delivery Charge base unless iZinga notifies the Referral Partner otherwise in writing.

2.4 Only one Referral Partner may earn commission in respect of any one referred furniture customer. Attribution is determined under clause 3 of this Agreement.

Schedule 1 — Clause 3: Food Store Partner Referral

3.1 The Stage 1 commission (R100.00) is earned on the date iZinga confirms in its system that the referred store is approved and live on the iZinga food delivery platform.

3.2 The Stage 2 commission (R150.00) is earned on the date the referred store's first customer order is confirmed as completed and paid in iZinga's system.

3.3 The Referral Partner's scope of assistance for store partner referrals is limited to:

(a) introducing the store to the iZinga business platform (biz.izinga.co.za);

(b) assisting the store owner in completing the store registration process, including setting up menu categories, menu items, and item-level pricing;

(c) helping the store owner upload any documentation required by iZinga's store approval process.

3.4 The Referral Partner must not configure, suggest, or adjust delivery vehicle pricing, platform service fees, or delivery cost structures on biz.izinga.co.za on behalf of the store or on behalf of iZinga. These are set automatically by iZinga's platform.

3.5 The Referral Partner must not represent to a referred store that the Referral Partner is an employee, agent, or official representative of iZinga, or that the Referral Partner has authority to approve or guarantee the store's listing.
